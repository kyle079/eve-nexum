# Product

## Register

product

## Users

EVE Online wormhole players ranging from solo day-trippers to organized J-space corporations. Mixed experience levels: some migrating from Pathfinder/Tripwire, some new to mapping tools entirely. Context is multi-monitor gaming sessions, often long and intense during fleet ops, sometimes quick check-ins for chain status. They need density and speed without being intimidated on first use.

## Product Purpose

Free, open-source, self-hosted wormhole mapping tool that replaces legacy alternatives (Pathfinder, Tripwire, Siggy). Real-time collaboration for scanning chains, tracking intel, managing signatures and structures. Success looks like: corps self-host it, new members onboard quickly, experienced scanners feel faster than their old tool.

## Brand Personality

Bold, technical, alive. High-energy, data-forward, unapologetically nerdy. The tool radiates competence without being cold. Real-time updates and live presence create a sense of activity and momentum. Speaks to players who appreciate precision and capability.

## Anti-references

- **Legacy EVE tools**: No Pathfinder/Tripwire/Siggy aesthetic. No 2010s PHP-era cramped tables, inline styles, or dated UI chrome.
- **Generic SaaS**: No Stripe/Linear visual clone. No cream backgrounds, gentle gradients, or corporate-neutral palettes. Nexum lives in space, not in a startup pitch deck.
- **Gamified flash**: No gratuitous neon glow, particle effects, or trying-too-hard sci-fi. Substance over spectacle. The game itself provides the universe fantasy; the tool provides clarity.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed is the feature.** Every interaction should feel instantaneous. If something takes time, show progress, never block. Perceived performance matters as much as actual performance.
2. **Density earns its space.** Pack information tight, but every element must justify its presence. No decorative padding, no empty states that waste screen real estate. Scanners need data, not whitespace.
3. **Alive, not animated.** Real-time presence, live updates, and responsive state changes create energy. But motion serves information, not decoration. The UI breathes; it doesn't dance.
4. **Expert-friendly, newcomer-survivable.** Optimize for power users who live in the tool. Don't dumb down for beginners, but don't make onboarding a cliff. Progressive disclosure over simplified defaults.
5. **Distinct from everything.** Not a legacy tool reskin. Not a SaaS template. Not a game UI cosplay. Nexum has its own visual identity that players recognize immediately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best-effort approach. Prioritize high contrast ratios for long-session readability in dark environments, keyboard navigation for power users, and meaningful non-color indicators for status (icons, labels, patterns alongside color). No formal WCAG target, but don't ship anything that's obviously broken for colorblind or keyboard-only users.
